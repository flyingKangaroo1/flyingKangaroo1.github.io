Built a 1/10-scale autonomous race car with team SKKUDERIA_SKKAI for our first IFAC Roboracer 2026 appearance. Assembled the platform from a 2D SOSLAB GL5 LiDAR, a Traxxas Slash chassis, and an NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, and implemented the full perception–planning–control stack.
