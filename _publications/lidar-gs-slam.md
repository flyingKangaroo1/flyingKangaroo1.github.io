A real-time LiDAR Gaussian Splatting SLAM system that couples fast G-ICP tracking with spherical rasterization-based mapping, reusing tracking covariances to drive range-aware Gaussian initialization, surface normal estimation, and complexity-based pruning/densification. It achieves 86.78% F-score on Newer College at >20 FPS with purely online trajectories.
